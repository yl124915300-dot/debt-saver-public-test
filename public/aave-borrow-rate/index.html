Why rates can diverge

Borrow costs across Aave, Morpho, Compound, Spark and other lending markets can differ because liquidity, utilization, collateral markets and risk parameters differ. A lower headline APY is not enough: a refinance only makes economic sense when expected savings exceed flash liquidity, gas, slippage, protocol and service costs over the expected remaining debt life.

What the public test does today

The live public test can read supported Morpho Blue debt on Ethereum. It intentionally fails closed when the comparison source is unavailable, rather than manufacturing a savings quote. A reviewed historical demo is available to show the quote format.

Debt Saver is experimental software, not financial advice. Savings estimates, when available, are not guarantees and must be refreshed before any future executable workflow.
