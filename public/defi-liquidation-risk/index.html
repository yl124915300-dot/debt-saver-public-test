What a safe refinance check should include

  • Current debt and effective borrow APY
  • Alternative route after execution costs
  • Post-migration health factor
  • Break-even time vs expected remaining debt life
  • Slippage, flash liquidity and fee assumptions

A lower rate does not automatically reduce liquidation risk, and refinancing can introduce its own risks. Debt Saver therefore fails closed when it cannot verify a comparison path.
